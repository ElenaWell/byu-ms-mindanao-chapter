The BYU Management Society Mindanao Chapter brings people together to grow professionally, lead with integrity, and make a meaningful difference in their communities.

OUR CHAPTER

Who We Are

The BYU Management Society Mindanao Chapter is part of a global community committed to developing moral and ethical leaders. Through professional relationships, learning opportunities, and shared experiences, we seek to help individuals become better professionals and more purposeful contributors to society.

Rooted in Mindanao, our chapter aims to connect individuals across industries and generations, creating opportunities for growth, collaboration, and service.
